About Me

Hello! I'm Champaigne Escultura, a BTLED-ICT student at the Technological University of the Philippines. As a student who often needs to print assignments, projects, and other school requirements, I created this guide to help fellow students find nearby print shops that provide convenient and affordable services.

Why I Chose This Topic

I chose this topic because I experienced the challenge of looking for reliable and affordable printing services as a student. Printing is a common need for college students, especially when submitting assignments, projects, and other academic requirements. Through this guide, I want to help fellow TUP students save time and easily find print shops that can support their school needs.

Why Trust My Recommendations?

My recommendations are based on the needs of students who are looking for convenient, accessible, and budget-friendly printing services. I considered factors such as location, usefulness, and the type of services that students commonly need. This guide aims to provide helpful information from a student's perspective to make choosing a print shop easier.
